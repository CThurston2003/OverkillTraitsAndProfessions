# OverkillTraitsAndProfessions

Overkill Traits And Professions (OTAP) is a passion project mod for the game Project Zomboid. 
This mod aims to add a plethora of new Traits and Professions that you can choose for your character.

This project is written in Lua, as thats what's used for Project Zomboid modding


## Motivation
This mods goal is to give the player a lot more choice when it comes to making a character with more niche idiosyncrasies and oddball traits about them. 
The hope is to have simple traits ranging from the fear of the dark (Nyctophobia) all the way up to things like allowing the player to eat zombie corpses (if prepared correctly). 
This mod is very early in it's development, and it also serves as a sort of proof to myself that I can get at least *something* onto the steam workshop, even if it's not a fully fleshed out project or mod.

This mod is a passion project put together by me in order to gain more experience with programming in the context of game development and design, as it's a passion of mine, and one I hope to nurture and grow as time goes on. And as such, at the end of it all, this mod is a learning experience and I want player feedback so please feel free to test and mess around with this mod! I also welcome suggestions and/or reports of bugs that might be present in the codebase.

## Prerequisites 
In regards to steam mods, this mod is fully standalone and requires no others

## Installation
- Go to the Steam Workshop page here: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3814389738
- Click the "Subscribe" button
- Enable in your mods in game

## Usage
Choose your desired traits and/or profession and enjoy!

## Contributing
As this is just a personal passion project of mine, I prefer all work to be done personally or by people I know personally. 
That said, I am always open to suggestions regarding new traits and professions


## Currently Implemented Traits
- Nyctophobia: Gets panicked in dark spaces
- Astraphobia: Gets panicked from thunderstorms
- Nyctophilia: Calms down and enjoys the darkness
- God (Testing trait): God trait to max out stats for testing purposes left in for fun
